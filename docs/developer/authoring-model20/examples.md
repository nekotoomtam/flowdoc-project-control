only: Core0.6.0 / Service0.6.0 / schema029 and its exact reviewed archive.

and request.json (CLIS has request-20.json/request-60.json). Section examples also
have preview-body.json. Definitions and requests must be used as a pair.

identity and sampleRequest.docKey accordingly. Keep definition.version1. Select

example is not proof that your current resource catalog can render it. Preserve
all content and geometry; inspect input, warnings and actual PDF/Word output.
