# Root and V3 Transition Contracts

## Authority and Scope

This leaf owns the bounded chronology and current executable evidence for the
accepted 5A retained root, the 5B Root V2 transition, the scoped V3 corrective
policy, and their source-envelope limits. Its 10 assigned sources are
unchanged between frozen Core commit `76a2f2311a898e781f53773390d47b05812911e4`
and current evidence commit `c503a45c03e0ce3b7a6efba2b029ca842017faa0`:
**10 assigned / 10 unique / 0 missing / 0 extra / 0 drift**. This leaf does
not own later 5B-2 transaction internals or generic product activation.

## Responsibility Boundary

This leaf owns the transition from the 5A retained-root checkpoint to the
bounded Root V2/scene/delivery lane, its deterministic work policy, the V3
source envelope, and explicit stop boundaries. It does not broaden persistent
flow/range ownership, transfer 5B-2 authority from downstream documentation,
or promote a scoped review result into Editor, Backend, publication, or
production authority.

## 5A Retained Root

Phase 5A is the accepted Core-only retained-root checkpoint: construction is
all-or-blocked over its exact dependency chain and produces a data-only scene
with false product flags. It establishes neither an incremental transition nor
the later V3 policy. 5A acceptance does not activate V3 policy. Its complete
scene projection and fixture observations remain bounded evidence, not product
latency or binding claims.

## 5B Root V2 Admission

The active 5B-1 lane admits Root V2 true no-op and inline-image paint
transition only. A true no-op preserves the exact prior Root and scene wrappers;
the paint transition proves its selected subtree reuse under the exact active
policy. This admission does not authorize text/style, semantic-only,
authored-box, fixed-height/overflow, exclusion, 5B-2, or 5B-3 transition
capabilities.

## Scene and Delivery

Persistent Scene V2 separates structural-semantic identity from observational
payload facts. Scene delivery preserves canonical retain/cover and detached
complete-delivery validation under exact registered authority. Incremental
candidate, complete fallback, and complete oracle work remain separate ledgers;
the oracle is verification-only and does not choose an incremental path or
become production hot-path authority.

## Work Policy

The scoped V3 work policy has ordered factual rows with locked limits and
inactive future rows. Limits are deterministic, operation-owned, and based on
the named calibration evidence; wall-clock time and payload estimates do not
select execution. Inactive capability rows do not describe current product capability; they reserve future 5B-2/5B-3 work. A structural-calibration or
inactive-reference fixture similarly does not execute a transition capability.

## Source Envelope

For an accepted V3 Root V2, the source envelope is checked before source
binding and remains bound to exact process-local Root/policy authority. A
source-envelope breach blocks rather than creating a partial accepted candidate.
This is a bounded 5B-1 Root V2 acceptance invariant; it is not the later
5B-2 source-authority or source-commit transaction contract.

## V3 Corrective Scope

The V3 corrective work records factual operation counts, scoped fallback and
delivery integrity, and an explicit review stop for the 5B-1 lane. A V3 scoped PASS does not activate 5B-2 source authority. It confirms only its named
Root V2/scene/delivery policy range when present executable evidence supports
that range; it does not activate Worker sessions, Editor apply, Backend
persistence, publication, production, or retirement of older compatibility
shapes.

## Stop Boundaries

5B-1 stops for review before later 5B-2 text/style and 5B-3 image/spatial/scale
work. Current bounded evidence does not establish product-scale memory, latency,
fallback frequency, scheduling, Worker lifecycle, fixed-height/overflow,
Columns/Table, or product integration. Inactive capability rows are not current
product capability, and later planning is not authorization to cross this stop.

## State and Failure Model

Root V2 and its children are process-local and fail closed on invalid,
unregistered, stale, malformed, or authority-inconsistent input. Policy-limit
and proof failures use the bounded fallback protocol; no partial incremental
candidate enters complete construction. Accepted results preserve false
publication/production flags. Source, policy, scene, and delivery facts are
validated by their named owner rather than inferred from payload observation.
